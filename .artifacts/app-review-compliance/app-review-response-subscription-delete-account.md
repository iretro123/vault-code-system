Hello App Review,

We updated the app to address both issues from the previous review.

1. Subscription information

The in-app subscription purchase flow now displays all required subscription information before purchase, including:
- subscription title
- subscription duration
- subscription price
- functional Privacy Policy link
- functional Terms of Use / EULA link

These links are visible directly in the Vault OS subscription screen before the user starts the purchase.

2. Account deletion

We added a dedicated in-app account deletion flow at:

Settings -> Account -> Delete Account

The flow includes:
- a visible Delete My Account button
- a permanent deletion warning
- a final confirmation step requiring the user to type DELETE
- the live account deletion request
- sign-out after successful deletion
