# Vault OS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-16

Vault OS is operated by Vault Trading Academy LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how Vault OS and the related academy services collect, use, and protect information when members use the app.

## Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

- Account information such as your name, email address, profile details, and membership status
- User content such as chat messages, uploads, screenshots, profile images, and other content you choose to submit
- Progress and activity information such as lesson progress, onboarding state, check-ins, feature usage, and notification preferences
- Device and app information such as device identifiers used for login sessions and push notifications
- Support information you provide when requesting help
- Billing or access-related status needed to confirm your membership or product access

Vault OS does not ask users to provide bank account credentials or brokerage passwords inside the app.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- create and manage your account
- deliver academy content, lessons, and community features
- provide chat, notifications, and live session updates
- personalize onboarding, progress, and in-app reminders
- verify access, billing status, and membership entitlements
- respond to support requests
- protect the app, investigate abuse, and maintain security

## Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, Vault OS may send you alerts for activity such as:

- mentions
- community updates
- live session notices
- announcements
- new modules or lesson-related updates
- motivation or admin messages

You can manage notification settings from your device and from in-app settings where available.

## Sharing and Service Providers

We may use service providers to operate parts of the app, such as authentication, storage, analytics, payment or membership verification, messaging, and notification delivery.

Information is shared only as needed to operate the service, secure the platform, process access or billing workflows, and communicate with members.

## Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the app, provide academy services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Content or account information may be removed or anonymized when no longer needed, subject to operational and legal requirements.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect account information and user content. No system can guarantee absolute security, and users should also protect their own devices and credentials.

## Children

Vault OS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the app.

## Your Choices

You may be able to:

- update parts of your profile in the app
- manage notification permissions on your device
- contact support regarding account questions or data-related requests
